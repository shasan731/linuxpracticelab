# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Linux Practice Lab is a learning project. Participation should make it easier for people of
different backgrounds and experience levels to learn, contribute, and ask questions.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being patient and specific when explaining technical decisions;
- giving actionable, respectful feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and different learning needs; and
- focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication
of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/shasan731). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, previous behavior, and willingness to repair harm when
choosing a response.
